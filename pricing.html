Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our memberships.

Can I cancel my membership at any time?

Yes, all our monthly memberships are commitment-free. You can cancel at any time with a 15-day notice before your next billing cycle.

Do classes roll over to the next month?

Classes in the Foundation and Balanced plans must be used within the billing month and do not roll over.

Do you offer student or corporate discounts?

Absolutely! Please reach out to us via the contact page with your details to learn about our special rates.
